Why we make it

Residents arrive in cardiothoracics with a lot to learn and very little protected time to learn it. A 20-minute episode fits a commute, a walk to the car park or the gap between a list finishing and a handover starting. We cover what the induction talks cover, and then what they don't: the tacit knowledge of a national transplant and ECMO centre, the reasoning that never makes it into the textbook.

Most subjects come as a pair: the intensive care half and the theatre half. Where we quote thresholds, doses and practice, they are Wythenshawe's at the time of recording. Take the principles and check your own guidelines.

The podcast is free and stays free. No sponsors, no advertising, no paywall, and neither of us is paid for it. Views are our own and not those of Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust.
